Our Auckland Art Gallery Visit

The Auckland Art Gallery's innovative approach to this exhibition showcases "Picasso: Designed by Paul Smith". A deeply personal and curated experience, merging raw geometric forms with bold, contemporary patterns that transform the viewer's experience.

It is an awe-inspiring collection showcasing the genius of Picasso — you leave wanting to buy canvas and paint to embark on your own creative journey, hence the inspiration to document the experience for others to see!

— Brian & Monya

1

Colour flooding

Periods become walls: inky blue for melancholy, magenta for the Pink Ladies, charcoal for wartime, forest green for the late years.

2

Pattern & motif

Signature Paul Smith stripes frame the striped sitters of the 1930s; a yellow-and-grey harlequin grid echoes Spanish bullfighting.

3

Tactile worlds

Artificial grass underfoot for Le Déjeuner sur l’herbe, vintage wallpapers for the collages, and walls flyposted with old exhibition posters.
